## 1. Reviewer criteria

A candidate should satisfy:

1. **Independence** — not the author; no prior involvement in AetherIntent.
2. **Relevant expertise** — in at least one of: distributed systems, formal methods, AI safety, multi-agent systems, or policy enforcement.
3. **No conflict of interest** — no financial or institutional stake in the outcome.
4. **Willingness to sign** — the review must be signed and dated (T1, T2).

## 3. What the reviewer is asked to do

Read `INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_PROTOCOL.md` and follow it. The review should assess:

1. The central question (is it well-posed? falsifiable?).
2. The assumptions A1–A6.
3. The definitions D1–D3.
4. The propositions P1–P2.
5. The failure conditions II-F1–II-F4.
6. The boundaries with Policy / Audit / Govern.
7. The threats T1–T7.

The reviewer is **not** asked to agree with the author. A review that concludes "the classification (E) is correct" and a review that concludes "the classification should change" are both valid.
